# Medical Policy: Advanced Imaging — Lower Extremity MRI

**Policy ID:** POL-MSK-IMG-014
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Status:** Synthetic example for ClaimSight portfolio project.

## Coverage statement

Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the lower extremity joints, including the knee (CPT 73721, 73722, 73723), is covered when criteria for medical necessity are met as defined in the corresponding clinical guideline (CG-MSK-073721-001).

## Prior authorization

Prior authorization is **required** for:

- All advanced imaging studies (MRI, CT) for non-acute musculoskeletal conditions
- Repeat imaging of the same anatomic site within 12 months of a prior advanced imaging study, unless documented change in clinical status

Prior authorization is **not required** for:

- Imaging performed in the emergency department
- Imaging within 30 days of a documented acute traumatic injury

## Documentation requirements

Claims must include:

- Specific ICD-10 diagnosis code(s)
- Date and result of prior plain film of the same anatomic site (within 12 months)
- Documentation of conservative therapy when applicable per clinical guideline
- Provider attestation of medical necessity

## Coverage limitations

- One MRI of the same joint per 12-month period unless documented change in clinical condition
- Bilateral imaging counts as a single study only when both joints are imaged in the same session and clinically indicated

## Member cost-sharing

Subject to plan benefits. For PPO plans, advanced imaging is typically subject to deductible and coinsurance. Refer to member's specific benefit summary.
